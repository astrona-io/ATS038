# Summary

NetworkPolicy selects Pods and defines allowed connections in isolated traffic directions. Allowances combine across policies, and both endpoints' policy can affect a connection. Enforcement depends on the network implementation.

A service mesh can add communication identity, encryption, routing and telemetry. TLS needs trusted certificate verification, and mutual TLS authenticates both peers. Authorization and safe retry behavior remain separate application and policy requirements.
