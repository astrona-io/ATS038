# Summary

The pattern of affected workloads helps identify shared node, networking or storage dependencies. Conditions and pressure reports are evidence that needs interpretation, while controllers still need replacement capacity after eviction.

A Service request crosses discovery, endpoint selection, forwarding and application execution. Check each stage and preserve the affected caller's context. A successful early stage narrows the problem without proving later stages work.
